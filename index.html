How to play

  • Phone: thumb the invisible stick in the bottom-left — claw moves by stick direction (velocity). Finger stays off the scrap.
  • Desktop: mouse-hover aims the claw, or hold WASD / arrow keys.
  • Power is a timed cycle. Return to the Nest to spend parts.
  • Upgrade Map nodes are buy-once. Grow claw size, shred, magnet, spawn, auto-claw.
  • Nest Anchor unlocks Nest Reset — spend Relics on permanent buffs and helper bots.
  • Each helper bot has its own skill-tree branch.
